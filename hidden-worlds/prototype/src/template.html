How it works

  1. The seed picks the targets, a set of lookalike decoys in other colours, and the filler objects.
  2. Each target goes either in the open or partly behind a tree, bush or building.
  3. The engine stacks a mask of every sprite in draw order, so it knows which sprite is on top at every pixel.
  4. Any target that shows less than the difficulty allows is moved, and the check runs again.
