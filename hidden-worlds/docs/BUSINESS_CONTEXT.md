# Business context

Read this once. It explains the choices in the spec.

## What v1 is for

v1 is a test, not a launch. It goes on the public web for four to six weeks to answer one question: **do strangers come back on day 7?** The pass mark is 12% of new players; under 8% after two rounds of changes ends the project. Everything else (apps, payments, ads, more art) is funded only if that number is good.

So v1 optimises for, in order:

1. A stranger on a phone is playing within seconds, with no sign-up.
2. They want to come back tomorrow: a new puzzle each day, a streak, someone new to find, a world to unlock.
3. They can share a result or challenge a friend in two taps.
4. Every one of those is measured with clean, anonymous events.
5. It never breaks, on any phone, on a bad connection.

## The business in one paragraph

Free daily puzzle. Later: a Premium subscription (planned $4.99 a month) for unlimited hints, the full archive and all worlds; optional rewarded ads for extra hints; paid world packs. In v1 none of that is live. The Premium screen is an honest interest test: it says Premium is not available yet and counts who wants it.

## Who plays

The genre's most dedicated players are women over 55, on phones and tablets. That means: large touch targets, readable text, no time pressure in the scoring, a relaxed mode, forgiving taps, calm motion, nothing that feels like a trick.

## Art

The art in v1 is placeholder vector art from the prototype. Commissioned art arrives later as a sprite pack: a human illustrator designs the style and characters, and sprites are produced inside that style with AI assistance and hand editing. The game must accept a new pack as data with no code change. The About screen discloses how the art is made.

## Constraints from the owner

- The business must run without daily attention. Puzzles generate from the date; nothing needs publishing each day.
- The look matters. A polished, distinctive interface is a requirement, not a nice-to-have.
- The name is a working title and may change.
- General-audience product. Collect no personal data in v1.
